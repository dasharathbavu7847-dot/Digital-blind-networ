About Digital Blind Network

Digital Blind Network is an accessible digital platform designed to help blind and visually impaired users access useful digital tools.

Our goal is to make technology more accessible, useful, and convenient for everyone.

Help Menu

Text to TXT File Help

Enter or paste your text, choose a file name, and select Download TXT File.

Image Resizer Help

Select an image, enter the desired width and height, choose a format, and resize it.

Image Reduce Help

Select an image, adjust image quality, choose maximum width, and reduce its size.

Audio Editing Help

Upload audio, enter start and end times in seconds, and trim the selected audio.

Video Editing Help

Upload video, use rewind or forward controls, enter start and end times, and export the selected video section.
