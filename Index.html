About Digital Blind Network

Digital Blind Network is designed to provide accessible digital tools for blind and visually impaired users.

Our goal is to make technology easier to use through accessible controls, screen-reader-friendly interfaces and useful digital tools.

Accessibility

The website supports labels, keyboard navigation and screen-reader announcements wherever possible.
